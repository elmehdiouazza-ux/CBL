⚠️ Microphone blocked

Your browser blocks microphone access for files opened directly from your phone. Here is how to fix it:

  1. Open Chrome on your phone
  2. Go to the address bar and type:
    chrome://flags/#unsafely-treat-insecure-origin-as-secure
  3. Enable that flag and add file:// as allowed
  4. OR — easiest: upload this file to any website (e.g. GitHub Pages) and open via https://

💡 The spreadsheet works without mic — you can still type manually in every cell.
